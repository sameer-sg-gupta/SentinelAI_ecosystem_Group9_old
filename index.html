    A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

    Your questions.
    Answered clearly.

    Who pays for SentinelAI?

    The proposed model is insurer-funded platform access. Participating manufacturers would not pay a separate software subscription, but still need to resource their own corrective actions, staff time and any production interruptions.

    Does this replace inspections or risk engineers?

    No. The proposed platform supports preparation and follow-through between inspections. Qualified specialists retain responsibility for engineering decisions and the agreed verification scope.

    Will the pilot reduce our insurance premium?

    No premium credits are included in the proposed pilot. Future incentives would require insurer agreement and supporting evidence. No savings or loss-reduction outcome is guaranteed.

    Why involve an independent verifier?

    Detection and recommendation should be separate from assurance. An independent specialist checks selected implementations against an agreed scope and records remaining deficiencies. This makes the prevention record more credible without claiming that every risk has been eliminated.

    Are cameras used to monitor employees?

    The concept focuses on machinery, processes and corrective actions, not individual performance. Any video collection, permitted uses and access rights must be agreed with the site and relevant employee representatives before the pilot.

    What information would a pilot use?

    A jointly agreed selection of maintenance records, inspection findings, incident histories and action evidence. Periodic video may be included where appropriate and agreed. Data access and permitted uses must be defined before onboarding.
